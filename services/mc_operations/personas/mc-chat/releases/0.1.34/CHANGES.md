# 0.1.34 — slim base persona, procedures as skills (DRAFT for Robert's review)

12 November 2026 · Claude Code · #553 step 1.

- **Why.** OpenClaw sends the workspace persona files on every turn. In 0.1.33 that was 21.4k characters (AGENTS projection 15.9k with the handbook's SKILL.md pasted in, SOUL 3.2k, and OpenClaw's two empty starter templates). Every persona change also invalidates every cached prefix. Robert's direction (#553, 12 Nov): a small byte-stable base of identity and core rules; procedures looked up when needed.
- **Base (sent every turn).** SOUL.md and AGENTS.md together are 6,115 characters (0.1.33: 13,784). AGENTS keeps the contract's core rules (evidence, honesty, security; "say briefly what you can't retrieve" is an honest pass until recall exists), "How to answer" (short answers, job health), and a short skills section that names which card to open for which kind of message, plus two rules that must hold before any card is read: corrections are capture events, and the judgment store is off limits. Revision after review (#553): the receipt, Board and dispatch rules moved verbatim to mc-building, and the "Cards loaded" opener stays only in mc-corrections. SOUL keeps identity, voice and the language rule, with duplicates of AGENTS lines removed, and says the partner stance plainly (recommend first, correct him, hold a view until given a better reason, then go with his decision; the voice card's words, Robert 6 Nov), and a sense of humor fit for long-time partners (Robert, 12 Nov review: dry and warm, never at the cost of a clear answer, absent when he is frustrated or something is broken).
- **Skills (read on demand).** Each dated procedure moved verbatim into `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`; only its name and one-line description sit in the prompt: mc-front-door (Front door, 8 Nov), mc-building (Work depth and ownership, 10 Nov; the contract's build/deploy and review lines; Build supervision, 26 Oct), mc-delivery (What was delivered, 7 Nov, with the GitHub runs line), mc-guildhall (Guildhall, 12 Nov), mc-corrections (Correction capture). The handbook's engineering SKILL.md is now the minimoi-engineering skill instead of being pasted into AGENTS (`handbook.load: "skill"`); handbook 0.1.3 itself is unchanged.
- **Nothing lost.** Every rule of 0.1.33 is either in the base or verbatim in a skill (tests/mc_persona/test_persona_0134.py checks this line by line). Lines that said the same thing twice (SOUL and AGENTS) are kept once.
- **Cap.** The AGENTS projection check is 8,000 characters (was 16,000) in start.sh and check 6c, so dated sections can't grow back.
- **Injected every turn,** measured on OpenClaw 2026.9.6: 8,317 characters in the Workspace Files section (AGENTS projection with RUNTIME binding and handbook pointer, SOUL, file headers, and OpenClaw's one-line IDENTITY.md marker). 0.1.33 in production: 21,449.
- **Opening a card on every route (Robert, 12 Nov: one mechanism for all models).** AGENTS tells MC to open a card with `minimoi_open_card` (new read-only MiniMoi tool, `mc.skill.read`, tier 1) and lists the cards with when to open each, so the index is visible even on a route that doesn't inject OpenClaw's skills list (the Claude plan route turns skills into Claude's own Skill tool, which MC's policy denies).
- RUNTIME binding (1,153 characters: container paths, tool naming, file hand-off, credential paths) and the voice card are byte-identical to 0.1.33; each RUNTIME line is a security or tool-naming rule, so it stays. No new tool, permission or authority. `mc-building` is a broad card for now; split it later.
