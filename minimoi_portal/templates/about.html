1.2 beta, November 2026.

mini-moi is a personal AI agent platform. It carries context across reading and research, language learning, and the work of building and running the system itself. It remembers my interests, what I have already investigated, how my thinking develops, what I decide to follow up on, and what I got wrong.

The same pattern applies beyond one person. Persistent context and feedback can help teams, organizations, and their systems learn and improve over time. This repository is the personal version.

mini-moi has been in daily use since February 2026 and on AWS since June 2026. Release 1.2 went to production as a beta on 9 November 2026. This repository documents the system as it works today, with current capability kept distinct from planned work. Where the 1.2 end state is ahead of production, I say 1.2 (beta) and name the open issue.

Current state (1.2 beta, November 2026)

Five user-facing domains. Two are partner domains, formalized in 1.2, where agents and I work on the platform and on my own priorities. Three are the learning domains the platform grew from.

Domain What it does today
Guild Design and build. Master Craftsman (MC), the main agent of my Workshop, works here and is live in production by chat, and by voice as a lighter walk until voice parity in 1.2 (beta) (#659). It coordinates the coding agents that build mini-moi, judges what comes back, and acts only through mini-moi's tools under a policy I approved.
Chief of Staff My thinking partner across all of mini-moi, live in production by chat and voice. It keeps my goals, plans, decisions, reminders and a Board, watches on a schedule, and pushes back.
Curator Daily reading and sustained research. Each morning it ranks about 700 candidates from international RSS feeds, X bookmarks, and theme-driven web search down to 20, with a serendipity reserve against a closed loop. Selected articles become investigations, deeper dives with a separate challenger pass, and a thinking record. Research intelligence lives here.
Mein Deutsch German immersion that began with a trip to Vienna and no chance to speak beforehand. AI personas I write, in everyday scenes, hold unscripted voice conversations adapted to what I can manage. Reading (Lesen) and writing with correction feed the same loop, and each session shapes the next.
Meu Português The same design for Brazilian Portuguese, for a family with different levels. Each person keeps a separate learning record; nobody is sorted into a level.

Records and Guildhall are foundations, not domains: Guildhall is the room where I meet both partners, and Records keeps those meetings.

What is new in 1.2

  • Two AI partners, live in production. Master Craftsman and Chief of Staff, each on its own runtime, reachable by chat and voice, each acting only through its own tools bound to my turn.
  • Agent coordination. Guildhall, where MC, CoS and I meet in one room; a helper for each partner under a charter I approved; delegation to the coding agents only on my exact approval; and one approval model, a card with the exact text and a one-time code, with a receipt kept.
  • Memory that every participant builds. Builder sessions, partner conversations and chat exports land on one file-first shelf, the Vault. A source captures nothing until I approve it, and sync never deletes.

Governance you can check, model choice without lock-in, and voice with the same power as text round out the release. The README has each in full.

Production is one modest AWS EC2 instance, with Cloudflare in front through a tunnel to the host. MC is its own container stack, deployed on a release tag. Dev and staging run on a second, smaller instance. Backups run daily, local and encrypted off-site, with a weekly second copy.
