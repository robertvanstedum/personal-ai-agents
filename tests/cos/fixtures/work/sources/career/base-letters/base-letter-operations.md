# Base letter — operations roles

I have spent fifteen years on the operations side of freight and analytics
businesses, and the through-line is reconciliation: making the number a
system reports and the number a warehouse lives agree with each other.

At Halden Freight Systems I rebuilt the exception desk and throughput rose
34% on completed loads. The mechanism was unglamorous — read the tickets,
name the pattern, change the schedule.

What I would want to understand about your operation is where the plan and
the week disagree today, and who currently absorbs that gap.
