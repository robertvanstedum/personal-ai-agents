# Planning Studio charter

Version 0.2 · 19 September 2026 · Owner-directed governance revision; publication PR final-text review pending

## Ownership and purpose

Robert explicitly places Planning Studio under **Chief of Staff**. It is a durable place for personal and cross-domain thinking, research, proposals, drafts and planning—not an exclusive folder that absorbs every domain's artifacts. CoS owns its continuity, intake and organization. Each initiative may have a distinct steward and multiple domain associations.

Guild supplies technical review, build quality and promotion standards. Guild does not own Planning Studio. Guild's Workshop is the implementation area; Guild's Prototype Lab is the experimentation area. A new service or subdomain is not required for either conceptual area.

Robert remains the decision point for adopting direction, approving reviewed implementation, promotion, public release and production changes. Neither CoS nor Guild independently grants those approvals. Domain ownership does not grant blanket access, transfer permission or authority to rewrite historical sources.

## Record rules

Preserve sources separately from interpretations. Authored source/design files remain canonical versioned artifacts. Accepted recorded-session events and Records notes are authoritative in the Records transactional store and produce readable versioned JSON/Markdown. Do not treat those derived exports as a second writable original. Explicit links join both classes into one logical collection; no unapproved migration is implied.

Keep stable IDs, versions, provenance, access and disposition. Correct through new records/versions rather than overwrite history. Deliberate capture is distinct from ordinary ephemeral conversation. Agent drafts and reported rationale remain attributed; no hidden reasoning or invented missing conversation is preserved as speech.

## Lifecycle and reuse

Retain the [record lifecycle vocabulary](RECORD_LIFECYCLE_v0.1_2026-09-02.md): sources, captures, initiatives, proposals, reviews, decisions, patterns and promotions. A pattern extracts a reusable portion, not the whole private initiative. A promotion links an approved exact version to its destination; it does not itself authorize implementation or public publication. Superseded sources remain available with disposition.

Backup, export, synchronization and promotion are independent contracts with explicit evidence and dependencies. Source transfer is default-deny until classification and approval satisfy the [current Records contract](../../docs/specs/minimoi-connected-work/RECORDS_ROOMS_v6.md). Private content remains separate from reusable public code. The link is relative to the repository structure; see the current direction below for the normative package.

## Scope of supersession

This edition replaces the Guild-ownership/lifecycle authority formulation in v0.1 and clarifies its §6 file-authority rule by record type. It preserves source/interpretation separation, cross-domain stewardship, human approval, privacy and portability. The old charter is historical, not a competing current owner assignment. It does not replace unrelated domain contracts or initiate a physical relocation.
