# Decisions — Mini-moi Work Proof of Concept

## 2026-09-05 — Register in Prototype Lab

Robert placed this work in Guild's Experiment area and its Prototype Lab. It is
a proof of concept inside the existing `try → prove → keep` process, not a new
domain, studio, or filing system.

## 2026-09-05 — Two deliverables

The experiment has two deliverables of equal standing in the build record:

1. the Mini-moi Work code and capability; and
2. the agent-and-Robert team process that designed, built, challenged, and
   reviewed it.

The case study is part of the second deliverable's artifact chain in the same
sense that vision, specification, implementation, tests, reviews, and release
evidence belong to the first. It is not merely later marketing copy.

The team-process deliverable may eventually become Master Craftsman material.
That promotion is deferred until repeated use shows what is durable.

## 2026-09-05 — Current maturity

The current label is **proof of concept**. It means the foundation and working
method exist and have survived independent review. It does not mean production,
real-use validation, or Chief of Staff integration is complete.

The next maturity decision follows file-level and real Career-use testing. If
Robert chooses `keep`, the capability moves toward a first private Chief of
Staff Work deployment, tentatively `v0.9`, followed closely by `v1.0` after
observed refinements.

## 2026-09-05 — Authority and boundaries

- Robert owns every promotion, release, merge, and external-action decision.
- Chief of Staff may draft and preserve work but may not submit an application
  or communicate externally without separate authority.
- Real Career sources and cover-letter bodies remain private.
- Products and models remain replaceable; the foundation is Mini-moi's.
- Prototype Lab is the durable tracked home for the proof of concept. It does
  not duplicate the codebase.
- The earlier Planning Studio folder is retained as design provenance. This
  project does not assume that Planning Studio will become a production system.
